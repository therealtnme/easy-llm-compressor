## Known limitations (keep documented, do not hide)
- EXACT_FUSE is usually UNSUPPORTED: transformer blocks contain a residual + a
  nonlinearity between candidate projections, so only `nn.Sequential` dense
  chains are proven fusible; block-level merges are refused with reasons.
- Beam-search cost is a heuristic proxy; real metrics are reported by validation.
- Non-uniform per-layer widths require the manifest loader, not plain
  `from_pretrained`.
- DATASET-FREE mode cannot distil and reports agreement/loss as unavailable.
- `scratch/` is gitignored (dev scripts only).
