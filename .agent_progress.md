## Known limitations (keep documented, do not hide)
- EXACT_FUSE is usually UNSUPPORTED: transformer blocks have a residual plus a
  nonlinearity between candidate projections, so only genuine `nn.Sequential`
  affine runs are provably fusible; block merges are refused with reasons.
- Beam-search cost is a heuristic proxy; real numbers come from validation.
- Non-uniform per-layer widths need the manifest loader (`load_compressed`),
  not plain `from_pretrained`.
- DATASET-FREE mode cannot distil and reports agreement/loss as unavailable.
- `scratch/` is gitignored (dev scripts only).
